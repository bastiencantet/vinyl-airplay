# Community conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels and equipment.
English and French are welcome. Be constructive, assume good faith, and make
technical disagreements about evidence rather than the person reporting them.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, spam and disclosure of another
person's private information are not accepted. Do not pressure contributors to
provide free installation support or immediate fixes.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads,
and restrict participation when needed. Decisions should be proportionate to the
behavior and explained when practical.

To report a concern, use a private GitHub reporting channel when available. If
none is available, request a private contact without posting personal details
in an issue. For software vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md.
